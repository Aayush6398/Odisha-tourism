Famous Temples

Shree Jagannath Temple Puri(ଶ୍ରୀ ଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ମନ୍ଦିର ପୁରୀ)

A sacred Hindu temple in Puri dedicated to Lord Jagannath, famous for the annual Rath Yatra.

Konark Sun Temple(କୋଣାର୍କ ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ ମନ୍ଦିର)

A 13th-century world heritage site built in the shape of a giant chariot with intricate stone carvings.

Shree Lingaraj Temple(ଶ୍ରୀ ଲିଙ୍ଗାରାଜ ମନ୍ଦିର)

The oldest temple in Bhubaneswar, showcasing the masterpiece of Kalinga architecture.

Famous Cuisine

Mahaprasad

Mahaprasad refers to the sanctified food offered to Lord Jagannath at the historic Jagannath Temple in Puri

Chenna Poda

Chenna Poda is a traditional Odia dish made with curd, jaggery, and cardamom, often served during festivals.

Pakhala Bhata

It is Soul food of every odia's during summer season. Pakhala Bhata is a fermented rice dish served with various accompaniments, popular during the summer months.

Famous beaches and Dams

Hirakud Dam

Hirakud is an industrial township and renowned tourist destination located in the Sambalpur district of Odisha, India

Golden Beach Puri

Golden Beach is one of the most popular beaches in Puri, known for its golden sand and scenic beauty.

Chilika Lake

Chilika Lake is the largest coastal lagoon in India and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Culture and Festivals

Rath Yatra

Rath Yatra is a grand festival celebrated in Puri, where the deities are taken out in a chariot procession.

Odissi Dance

Odissi is a classical dance form that originated in Odisha, known for its graceful movements and intricate hand gestures.

Dhanu Yatra

Dhanu Yatra is an 11-day annual, open-air mythological drama festival held in Bargarh, Odisha. Celebrated between December and January, it is recognized as the world's largest open-air theater in the Guinness Book of World Records.
